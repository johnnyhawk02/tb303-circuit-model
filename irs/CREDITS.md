# Impulse responses for the Conv reverb

48 kHz stereo 16-bit FLAC. Leading silence removed, trimmed to the -60 dB point (3 s at most) with a cosine fade over the last 25%, and scaled to equal energy (multiply by `gain` in irs.json to get unit energy per channel). The list, with character notes, is in `irs.json`.
