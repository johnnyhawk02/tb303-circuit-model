TB303 Circuit Model, macOS (Apple silicon and Intel)

TB303-Circuit-Model-plugins.zip holds VST3/ and Components/ (the AU). Unzip it straight into your
plug-in folder with one Terminal command (run it from the folder the zip is in):

  ditto -x -k TB303-Circuit-Model-plugins.zip ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/ && xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"TB303 Circuit Model.vst3" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"TB303 Circuit Model.component"

Then rescan plug-ins in your DAW. The plug-ins are signed ad hoc (not notarised), which is why the
xattr step is there: it tells macOS you trust this download.

TB303-Circuit-Model-app.zip is the standalone app, if you want it without a DAW.
