303 Circuit Model , by Johnny. Free and open source under the GNU AGPL v3, with no warranty. Source, licence, downloads and bug reports: github.com/johnnyhawk02/tb303-circuit-model.

Privacy. No accounts, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere. Your presets and settings stay in this browser (in the plug-in, in one file on your computer).

Trademarks. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Roland, Behringer or the makers of any gear it is modelled on. Product names belong to their owners and are mentioned only to describe the circuits.

Source. The original 1982 main board schematic, with values that sheet does not show clearly read from a modern clone's schematic. Parts on neither sheet are estimates. Running at 2× oversampling.

Pitch and VCO. A 6-bit R-2R ladder from 5.333 V gives 1/12 V per step. An exponential current charges C33 from 5.5 V to 12 V, then resets, smoothed with a polyBLEP.

VCF. Four coupled ladder stages with a tanh law per transistor pair, 18n bottom cap and 33n above, solved implicitly. Full feedback (k = 17) self-oscillates.

Envelopes and VCA. C62 decays through R138 + VR6 (200 ms to 2.5 s). Accent shorts VR6 and charges the sweep cap. The OTA VCA gets resonance compensation through C76/R100, and normal notes fade in through R_SOFT.

Mods. The Mods page parts map mostly to the controls of the best-known 303 mod kits: Normal and Accent decay, VEG decay, Soft attack, Slide time, Sweep speed and self-oscillating resonance.

Digital 224. Runs on its own 20 kHz clock with 12-bit gain-ranging converters, 16-bit words and a shared 32K-word delay memory. The core is Dattorro's published Griesinger-style plate network; the original unit's microcode is not public, so the hall, chamber, room and chorus variants are estimates.

Plate, Spring, Convolution. The other reverbs on the same slot: Dattorro's published plate as he gives it, a dispersive spring tank after Välimäki, Parker and Abel (stretched allpasses in a delay loop), and partitioned FFT convolution of real impulse responses (below) plus generated ones.

Convolution IRs. Plate impulse responses by Greg Hopkins (CC BY). Room, silo, garage, Musikverein and On a Star impulse responses from Voxengo, free for any use. Trimmed to 3 s at 48 kHz; see irs/CREDITS.md.

Rodent drive. LM308 stage with gain-bandwidth and slew limits from its 30p cap, 1N914 diodes to ground, then the 3.3n filter.
